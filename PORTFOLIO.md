# Portfolio choices

A focused collection of working systems is easier to evaluate than a large set of unfinished ideas. These three builds connect workflow discovery, data integration, product judgment, and operational decisions.

## Mission Control · delivery systems

Demonstrates traceable business metrics, a coherent domain model, uncertain planning, and a useful multi-view application. The product decision is when to unblock, reduce scope, or revisit a commitment. The technical challenge is preserving meaning across filters, hierarchies, state changes, and exports.

## Launch Decision Lab · product judgment

Demonstrates primary-metric discipline, assignment-quality checks, guardrails, experiment interpretation, and resource-constrained prioritization. A favorable estimate is not sufficient to ship. Hypothetical discovery and actual synthetic evaluation are kept distinct.

## Relay Operations · forward deployment and data engineering

Demonstrates integration of messy feeds into an auditable operational workflow: contracts, quarantine, deduplication, late events, lineage, replay, and constrained action selection. A small SQLite implementation makes the logic inspectable; it does not claim distributed lakehouse scale.

## Evidence to earn next

Conduct consented discovery with actual users, collect usability observations, identify a concrete task baseline, and compare outcomes. Publish only measurements actually obtained and disclose the method, sample, and limitations. Until that happens, the portfolio makes implementation and simulation claims only.
